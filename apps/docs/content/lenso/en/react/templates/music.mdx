## What is included

Music combines album browsing, search, library views and a player with a queue. In Duo, playback and navigation follow the active screen when you fold or unfold the device.

1. Find a track in Search and start playback.
2. Open Now Playing from the mini player. Try scrubbing, shuffling or opening the queue.
3. In Duo, fold the device while a track is playing and resume from the cover screen.
