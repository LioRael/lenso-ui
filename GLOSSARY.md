# Lenso UI language

## Collections and surfaces

**Content list (List)**: A collection of content rows. Rows may contain navigation,
descriptions, actions and independent controls.

**Option list (ListBox)**: A widget for choosing one or more options from a
collection. Its options share a keyboard focus and selection model.

**List editing**: A temporary mode for selecting content rows for batch actions
and changing their order. It preserves the content list's meaning.

**Surface**: The background, material and outer shape that visually group content.
It does not determine selection, navigation or ordering.

**Swipe actions**: Actions revealed beside a content row by horizontal movement.
They are separate from the row's selection and editing state.

**Refresh control**: An explicit request to reload the content of a scrolling view,
with progress and completion feedback.
